# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior data to uncover meaningful business insights using Python, MySQL, SQL, and Power BI.

The objective is to understand customer purchasing patterns, product preferences, discount usage, subscription behavior, revenue trends, and demographic influences on shopping decisions. The project follows a complete data analytics workflow from data cleaning and exploration to database management and dashboard visualization.

---

## Business Objectives

* Identify top-selling products and categories.
* Analyze customer demographics and purchasing patterns.
* Measure the impact of discounts on sales.
* Understand subscription behavior among repeat customers.
* Evaluate customer loyalty and purchase frequency.
* Determine revenue contribution across different age groups.
* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for decision-making.
